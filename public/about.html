What this is

Parlor is a small chat client for atproto Spaces. It is alpha code, written to test Spaces on the Bluesky alpha sandbox, and its first job is testing Clockwork Conductor, a D&D game master bot. Things will break, change, or vanish, especially when Spaces moves to public beta.
