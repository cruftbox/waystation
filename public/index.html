Parlor

Sign in

Parlor is a test client for atproto Spaces on the Spaces alpha sandbox. The sandbox is shared and Spaces content is not end-to-end encrypted: the people who run the sandbox can read it. Do not post anything private.

You sign in on your own server's page. Parlor never sees your password.

Hosting

    To host spaces, sign out and sign in again with "I want to host spaces" checked.
