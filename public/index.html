Waystation is a test client for atproto Spaces on the Spaces alpha sandbox. The sandbox is shared and Spaces content is not end-to-end encrypted: the people who run the sandbox can read it, and so can the operator of this Waystation server. Do not post anything private. Privacy details.

You sign in on your own server's page. Waystation never sees your password.
